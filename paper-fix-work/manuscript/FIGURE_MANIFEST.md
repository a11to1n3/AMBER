# Figure manifest

All manuscript figures are generated by `scripts/render_publication_figures.py`
with the shared settings in `scripts/publication_figure_style.py`. Vector PDF is
the authoritative manuscript format; 240-dpi PNG files are previews. Blue
denotes AMBER or snapshot/staged execution, amber denotes the comparator or
in-place execution, and charcoal/grey is contextual evidence. No renderer
removes or replaces an experimental observation.
